Intelligent Choice

Japani Steel Chaukhat vs. Traditional Wooden Chaukhat

Why modern architects, civil contractors, and smart homeowners in Uttarakhand are switching 100% to Japanese Steel Frames.

Japani Steel Chaukhat (श्री बालाजी)

Engineered Cold-Rolled Tata/Jindal Steel

  • 100% Immune to Termites & Pests

    Insects and white ants (दीमक) cannot penetrate steel. Zero pest damage forever.

  • Zero Warping or Swelling in Monsoons

    Retains laser-straight alignment regardless of heavy rain or Uttarakhand humidity.

  • 60% Lower Total Cost

    Costs roughly ₹2,000 - ₹3,500 per complete frame compared to ₹8,000 - ₹15,000 for wood.

  • Fire Retardant & Eco-Friendly

    Non-combustible metal helps prevent flame spread while saving natural forest trees.

  • Pre-Welded Hinges & Fast Masonry Installation

    Ready to install with heavy holdfasts, saving hours of tedious carpentry labor.

Traditional Wooden Chaukhat (लकड़ी की चौखट)

Teak, Sal, Sheesham or Pine Wood

  • High Termite Risk (दीमक का खतरा)

    High moisture areas cause termites to hollow out wooden frames from the inside.

  • Swelling & Jammed Doors in Rainy Season

    Wood absorbs atmospheric moisture and expands, causing doors to get stuck repeatedly.

  • Extremely Expensive Material & Labor

    Requires high grade seasoned wood, anti-termite chemical treatments, and expensive carpentry.

  • High Maintenance & Re-Polishing

    Needs frequent sanding, varnish, or oil polish every 2-3 years to prevent rot.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers about Japani Chaukhat pricing, gauge specifications, installation, and wholesale orders in Rudrapur.

Japani Chaukhat is a cold-rolled steel door frame engineered from high tensile steel sheets. Unlike traditional wood, it is 100% immune to termites (दीमक), does not warp or swell during rainy seasons, is fire retardant, lasts over 50 years, and is 50-60% more affordable than quality Teak or Sal wood frames.

In steel gauges, a lower number means a thicker sheet. 14 Gauge (2.0mm) is super heavy duty and ideal for main entrance doors and heavy security gates. 16 Gauge (1.6mm) is the standard builder choice for all bedroom and living room doors. 18 Gauge (1.2mm) is an economical option for bathroom flush doors and lightweight partition doors.

Prices are computed based on running feet and gauge thickness. At Shri Balaji Enterprises, single rebate chaukhats range between ₹110 to ₹185 per running foot, while double rebate (jali wala) chaukhats range between ₹145 to ₹240 per running foot. We offer up to 12% bulk discount for contractor and builder orders.

Japani Chaukhat comes with pre-welded MS hold-fast clamps on all three sides. As the mason (राजमिस्त्री) lays bricks, the clamps are embedded inside cement mortar (1:3 concrete grout) for indestructible structural anchoring. The frame back is filled with concrete to eliminate any hollow metallic sound.
